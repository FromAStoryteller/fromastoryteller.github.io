Privacy

This privacy statement explains how From A Storyteller may collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit or interact with this website.

Last updated: 27 May 2026

Privacy Statement

A simple overview

From A Storyteller is a creative website for stories, games, videos, tools, and other creative projects. You can browse most of the site without directly providing personal information.

If you choose to contact me, submit information through a form, or interact with features that collect information, that information will only be used for the purposes described in this statement.

Information collected

This site may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, or message content if you contact me or use a form.

Cookies and site data

Some features may use cookies, browser storage, or similar tools to help the site work properly, remember preferences, or support interactive features like games and tools.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to messages, operate the website, improve features, protect the site, and understand how visitors use the site.

Details

Information you provide

If you contact From A Storyteller directly, send an email, or submit information through a form, I may collect the information you provide. This may include your name, email address, and the content of your message.

This information is used only to respond to your message, manage the website, or complete the purpose for which you provided the information.

Information collected automatically

Like many websites, From A Storyteller may collect limited technical information when you visit the site. This may include information such as your browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, general usage activity, and basic server log information.

This information helps keep the website working, improve performance, understand what content is useful, and protect the site from misuse.

Games, tools, and browser storage

Some games or tools on this site may use your browser’s local storage to save settings, scores, preferences, or progress. This type of information is usually stored on your own device rather than being sent directly to me.

You can clear this information through your browser settings if you want to remove locally stored site data.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, improve performance, or understand general visitor activity.

If analytics tools are used, they may collect general information about how visitors use the site. This helps identify which pages, stories, games, tools, or features are useful.

Third-party services and embedded content

This site may link to or display content from third-party platforms, such as video platforms, social media, analytics providers, hosting providers, or other external services.

Third-party websites and services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. From A Storyteller is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Sharing personal information

I do not sell personal information. Personal information may only be shared where needed to operate the website, respond to your request, use trusted service providers, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and security of the site.

Keeping information safe

Reasonable steps are taken to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, or alteration.

However, no website or online service can guarantee complete security, so please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Accessing or correcting your information

You may ask to access or correct personal information that From A Storyteller holds about you. If you believe any information is incorrect, you can request that it be updated.

Changes to this privacy statement

This privacy statement may be updated from time to time as the website grows, new features are added, or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Questions

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, or would like to request access to or correction of your personal information, you can contact me by email.
