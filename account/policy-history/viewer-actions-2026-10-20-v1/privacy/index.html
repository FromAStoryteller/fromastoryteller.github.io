Privacy statement

A simple overview

You can read stories and use the site without an account. Anonymous audience insights are optional and collected only when you allow them. There are no advertising trackers. Hosting and external resources still involve network requests, and the games save limited data in your browser.

Who is responsible

From A Storyteller is a personally operated creative website based in New Zealand. From A Storyteller is responsible for information collected and held for this website. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests and complaints, email fromastoryteller@gmail.com.

Contact is by email. This notice covers fromastoryteller.com and correspondence about the site. It explains data practices; browsing or reading it does not give blanket consent to processing.

Information you choose to send

The Contact page provides an email address and links to social platforms. The site has no contact-submission form, newsletter subscription, public comments or payment checkout. Viewer accounts, when registration is available, are described below.

If you email me, I receive your email address, any name you provide, your message and attachments, and the technical information included with the email. I use this information to answer your enquiry, discuss a collaboration you request, handle a privacy request or resolve a related issue. You do not have to send this information to browse the site, but I may be unable to answer an enquiry without enough detail or a reply address.

Please send only what is needed for your enquiry and avoid sending sensitive information or other people’s personal details unnecessarily. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. Email is handled through Gmail, a Google service; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Website delivery and search

The site is delivered through Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare receives IP addresses, requested URLs, request headers and necessary security/operational information to deliver and protect the service. The source repository and build checks use GitHub. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Site search matches content in your browser. A search term can appear in the page address as a query parameter and in your browser history; loading or sharing that address can disclose the term to the hosting service or the person receiving the link. Do not enter confidential information into site search. The site does not send search terms to a separate analytics service.

Viewer accounts and profiles

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over. This is the site’s account rule. Public content remains readable without an account. Ordinary registration creates a viewer account and does not grant contributor or administrator access.

Creating an account requires an email address you control, a password, a chosen display name, a 16+ self-declaration, agreement to the identified Terms and acknowledgement that you have seen the Privacy notice. These details create, secure and operate your account. A legal name is not required; a profile bio is optional. If you do not provide the required details, you cannot register but can continue reading public content. No birth date or identity document is requested at signup. Self-declaration does not verify actual age.

Authentication services process password hashes, verification and recovery information, sessions and necessary security records. Passwords are not published or included in downloads. The server records the exact policy versions, document checksums, required confirmations and agreement time separately from editable profile information. Those personal records appear in your account-data download and are removed with your account. Earlier test-draft acknowledgements remain separate and are never treated as agreement to later published Terms.

The sign-in form offers Keep me signed in on this device, selected by default. When selected, the site stores the sign-in session in local storage for this browser and site, so other tabs and later browser visits can use it. It remains until you sign out, clear site data, the browser removes it or the service expires or revokes the session; no automatic inactivity sign-out is promised. Uncheck the option for a tab-only session. Tab storage normally ends when the tab closes, but browser session restoration may retain it. If persistent storage is unavailable, the site falls back to tab storage, then page memory if necessary. These choices store session credentials, not your password. Remembered tabs share the same sign-in and sign-out; sign out when using a shared device. Optional theme/game storage choices are separate from the sign-in choice. Recovery credentials remain only in memory while completing recovery. Do not share session credentials or private email links.

Your email is private account information. Your display name and optional bio may appear with published work. Do not put private contact details in those fields. Private drafts are accessible to their author and authorised staff for necessary operations, support, moderation or security. Profile-picture uploads are currently available only to contributors. Contributor images do not need prior approval. Published work, its cover and metadata, and public profile images are readable without an account. The selected publishing name, public profile, bio, welcome line and optional social links can appear on storyteller and content pages. Do not put private contact details in public fields.

Supabase provides authentication, database and uploaded-file storage. Primary FAS project data is stored in Sydney, Australia; support, security, subcontractor and other provider processing may occur elsewhere. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy.

Account verification, password recovery and necessary account/security emails are transactional, not a marketing subscription. Resend delivers authentication email through a verified sending domain with open and click tracking disabled. The sending region is Tokyo; Resend states that its account data, email metadata and logs are stored in the United States regardless of sending region. See Resend’s Privacy Policy. Correspondence to the contact address continues through Gmail. Provider records and their retention are distinct from the site-controlled account records described here.

Contributor work, images and draft recovery

Studio stores owned drafts, manuscripts, titles, descriptions, selected warnings and genres, tags, presentation settings, image descriptions, publication settings and technical versions. Required fields are identified in the editor. Account IDs, ownership, timestamps, versions and addresses are assigned automatically. These details provide editing, publishing, scheduling, accessible display, conflict detection and lifecycle controls. A changed title does not reset the stable content identity or automatically change an established public URL.

Uploads are checked for allowed type, size and image dimensions. Images can be cropped and normalised for display, with references and cleanup records used to replace or delete them safely. A file that you download from the service may be the stored processed image rather than the original camera file. Private originals are not exposed by a public Storage bucket. Public image access follows the associated content/profile visibility. Copies already downloaded by readers are outside this control.

Studio automatically keeps a recovery copy of unsaved text and editing metadata in this tab’s session storage, scoped to the signed-in account, for up to 24 hours from the copy’s save time. It is checked or removed when Studio reads it; the browser may retain expired bytes until the next visit or site-data removal. It is not encrypted separately from browser storage and is not a second cloud backup. Tab closure normally clears it, but browser restoration can retain tab data. Signing out through Studio or choosing to discard recovery removes the copy. Other sign-out paths and browser failures may leave a copy until cleanup or expiry. Session expiry can retain recoverable text; identity is checked before restoring it. You can download recovery files to your device, where you control their retention. Use a private browser profile on shared devices and clear site data when finished.

Warning requests include your account, requested label, reason and review status so staff can maintain the catalogue. Moderation and access actions keep the information needed to investigate and explain the action, including the affected record, actor, reason and time. We do not use private drafts for AI training. Read the Contributor policy for rights and publishing responsibilities.

Invitations and transactional email

An administrator may provide your email address to invite you to contribute. From A Storyteller, a personally operated website in New Zealand, collects and holds that address to deliver and manage the invitation, confirm the invited identity and prevent misuse. It is not a marketing subscription and does not itself create an account or accept any Terms. You can ignore an unwanted invitation or contact fromastoryteller@gmail.com to ask for access, correction or removal of the information held about you.

Administrators managing access, Supabase for account and invitation storage, and Resend for delivery receive the information needed for that purpose. The provider and international-processing details below apply. Invitation records include the recipient address, role, issuer, expiry and acceptance/revocation information. A private token is used to match acceptance; do not share it. The invitation link is removed from the address bar when opened, and can be retained in tab storage for up to 24 hours to continue after sign-in; expiry is checked when read. Browser restoration can retain tab storage.

Delivery operations store the recipient, invitation reference, job status, attempts, provider message reference and times for up to 30 days. Minimal signed delivery-event metadata is kept for seven days. The private sending credential in a queued invitation is removed after provider acceptance, cancellation or permanent failure, or the one-hour retry window. Invitation email open/click tracking is disabled. Delivery status concerns mail-server acceptance, not whether you read the email or where it appeared in your inbox. Provider-retained email metadata follows the provider’s notice. Contact messages remain separately in Gmail as described above.

Saved content versions

Studio retains up to 20 previous saved versions of published work from the introduction of version history, including text, editing metadata and uploaded game/tool source files and packaged assets. Each version records the associated content, owner and save time. These versions are private to the owner and authorised administrators editing shared FAS content. They support recovery from editing mistakes; they are not public archives or a guarantee of permanent backup.

New edits remove the oldest retained versions. Versions remain while the content and account exist and are removed from active systems when either is permanently deleted. They are included in owned-content downloads; individual versions can also be downloaded in Studio. Contact us for an export exceeding self-service limits. Restricted backups follow the 30-day deletion rules below. Restoring updates the saved content, and its public page if published, while retaining current ownership, address, cover and visibility.

Account downloads and deletion

Viewers and contributors can edit their profile and download a personal account-data ZIP. It contains readable account details and a JSON copy for software, including their registration and agreement history. It excludes passwords and sign-in credentials. Contributors can separately download their owned Community stories as text files and original images, with technical JSON in a metadata folder. Contact us if an export is too large or you need information outside the self-service download.

Deletion requires password verification and a separate final confirmation. It removes your account and its owned Community stories and uploaded images from active systems. Download what you want to keep first. Existing static games and stories not linked to your account are outside this scope. Staff accounts or records involving prior staff approvals can need a separately reviewed process; if self-service removal cannot finish, contact us to resolve it. Access and correction requests are also available by email.

Deleted personal data may remain in restricted backups for up to 30 days, including manually saved archives. It must not be returned to active use during recovery. A limited record may be kept separately only where needed for a specific legal obligation, dispute or security incident, restricted to that purpose and reviewed for deletion when no longer necessary.

Short-lived confirmation and request-limit records protect account actions. A minimal deletion record identifies the removed account, its removal status and dates so an older backup does not restore deleted material. Completed deletion receipts expire after 30 days; unfinished removal work must be resolved before its operational record is removed. These records do not contain the deleted stories, images or passwords.

Other people may have independently copied or archived public work; removal from this site cannot remove those copies. Providers may retain independently required records under their own notices. Account deletion is not a promise that every provider record is immediately erased.

Saved pieces, favourites and acknowledgements

If you choose to save a piece, add a game or tool to Favourites, mark a piece as read, played, watched, listened to or used, or select Loved it, we store that choice with your account ID, the content ID and update times. Saved lists are private and available across your signed-in devices. These are actions you request, separate from optional anonymous audience insights; disabling analytics does not disable your account collections.

Each choice can be undone. Contributors see only current aggregate acknowledgement and Loved it totals for content they own, without viewer names, emails or saved/favourite counts. These are voluntary acknowledgements, not verified completion or measured page views. Counts are not public and do not affect discovery or rankings. We do not send individual activity notifications.

Choices remain until you undo them, the linked content is permanently deleted, or your account is deleted. Unpublished or unavailable content is hidden from collections; saved choices may reappear if it is published again. Your choices are included in your personal account-data download. Supabase processes these records with the account services described above. Authorised operators may access necessary records for support, security or privacy requests; they are not public profile information.

Optional account saves and private multiplayer

Games that offer account features show an Enable account features button outside the game. You must sign in to a verified account and acknowledge the current policies before enabling them. Games without these features and the existing browser-only games remain playable without account saving.

Account saves contain your account ID, game ID, a named save slot, a version, the game’s defined progress/settings, a revision and an update time. Each game declares a restricted save format. Saves are private to your account and available across your signed-in devices. Game code can request only the current game’s saves after you enable it; it cannot receive your email, account ID, password or session credentials. Authorised operators may access retained data when necessary for support, security or privacy requests. Do not put sensitive information or another person’s details in game data.

Private multiplayer is available for FAS-published games. Creating or joining a room records the game and published version, host and participant account IDs, temporary player-seat IDs, participation/presence times and the game’s temporary player and shared state (such as moves, positions or scores). Other current room participants can see player-seat IDs and gameplay state; account IDs and emails are not shared with them. The host receives a private invitation code to share; the server stores a hash of the code. Anyone with the code and an eligible signed-in account can attempt to join while capacity allows, so share it privately. Rooms support two to eight players. They have no chat, voice, location tracking, public leaderboard or retained match history.

Rooms expire after two hours. Leaving removes your temporary state; a host leaving closes the room for everyone. Removed or changed game versions also close rooms. A cleanup task runs every five minutes and removes expired rooms and rooms whose host has been inactive for more than three minutes. Presence is checked during play. Brief operational request counters are retained for rate limiting, without a gameplay history. Account saves remain until you delete them or delete your account, including if the game is later removed. A game update may need a compatible save format; invalid or outdated writes do not overwrite the previous save.

Game saves lets you download or delete individual saves, download your own game data and leave rooms. Account-data downloads and account deletion also cover your saves and own temporary participation; they exclude other players’ private data and room invitation codes. Deleting your account closes rooms you host without deleting other players’ persistent saves. Restricted manual backups expire within 30 days under the backup process. Supabase processes these features with the account services described above. Downloaded starter projects use local demonstration data on your own device; these demonstrations are separate from live account saves.

Game data stored on your device

If you allow Game records, Brick Blitz saves your all-time high score and Rally Clash saves your best rally. If you allow Preferences, the site saves your chosen colour theme and Rally Clash saves sound and master-volume preferences. These values use local storage in your browser for this site. The site reads and updates these values only while their category is allowed. No account is required for local game records.

The site’s game code does not send these saved values to me or to an online leaderboard. They are specific to your browser and device and are not linked to a site account. Local storage has no automatic expiry in the current games: values remain until overwritten, their category is turned off, you clear them, or your browser removes them. Once storage permission expires, they are not read or updated without a new choice.

To remove them, open your browser’s settings for cookies and site data, find fromastoryteller.com and clear its stored data. This resets saved records and preferences. Blocking site storage may prevent saving and can affect game operation. Browser storage is subject to privacy rules too; describing it here does not itself obtain consent.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current site code does not set HTTP cookies and does not include advertising pixels, behavioural advertising, fingerprinting or session-recording tools. It uses the storage described above and optional anonymous audience insights described below. Ordinary browser caching and service-provider processing are separate.

The Cookies & site storage popup lets you allow optional storage, choose categories, or use essential storage only. Optional appearance/sound preferences and game records are not read or saved until you allow their category. Declining still lets you browse and play; current-page choices can remain in memory until you leave. Change your choice using Storage choices in the footer. Turning a category off removes its named saved values and stops future saving. Your choice is stored on this device for up to 180 days to honour it; clearing site data or an expired choice prompts you again. This does not delete account/cloud data. Anonymous audience insights have a separate optional choice. An earlier Accept all choice does not enable this new category; the popup asks you to choose again. Accepting Terms or browsing does not provide analytics consent. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals prevent this collection, even if the category was enabled. No advertising trackers are enabled.

Optional anonymous audience insights

When you allow audience insights, a visible public storyteller or content page sends its public page identifier, a broad source group (direct, internal, search, social or other) and a screen-size group to FAS through Supabase. We increment daily totals in UTC, with no visitor identifier, account ID, email, cookie, raw referrer, search terms or browsing history in the analytics record. Repeat views, including a contributor viewing their own page, can count. Known automated clients are filtered, but counts are estimates and do not represent unique people. We do not collect reading completion, time spent or gameplay activity for these reports.

Contributors can privately see and download aggregate reports for their own profile and owned content, covering up to 90 days. Publishing under the FAS name keeps reporting attributed to the original owning account. Public readers cannot see private insights. Collection begins with this feature; historical counts are not reconstructed. Anonymous daily totals are deleted by a daily cleanup task once they fall outside the 90-day reporting window. These non-identifying totals cannot be traced back to remove a particular visitor’s earlier view. Turning off audience insights in Storage choices stops future collection; it does not alter past aggregate counts.

The collection request still reveals ordinary network information such as an IP address and request headers to the service provider while delivering and securing it, as described under hosting and external services. FAS does not store those values in its audience reports. This choice does not disable essential page delivery, authentication or independent provider security logs. No new advertising service is connected.

Fonts, game libraries and external links

The site serves its MonteCarlo, Source Sans 3 and Literata fonts from its own assets. These font files do not require a separate request to Google Fonts.

Brick Blitz and Rally Clash load the p5.js and p5.sound libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare receives the network request information needed to deliver those files, including an IP address and request headers, and can process service logs. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. These game libraries are requested when the relevant page loads, independently of optional storage choices. A storage choice does not block network requests needed to deliver a page or game.

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are linked from the site. They are ordinary links, rather than embedded social feeds or video players in the current site. Following a link opens that provider’s service, where its own data practices apply. Their independent practices do not remove my responsibility for disclosures that I initiate.

How long information is kept

Site-controlled authentication and security audit records are kept for up to 30 days, with longer retention only for a specific ongoing incident or legal need. Any such exception is restricted to that purpose and reviewed for deletion. This is separate from account records needed while your account remains open and from records independently retained by service providers.

My retention rule is to keep correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and relevant follow-up. An ongoing collaboration, an unresolved complaint, a legal obligation or a genuine need to establish or defend a claim can justify longer retention. When those purposes no longer apply, I delete the correspondence. This is a purpose-based retention rule; there is no automatic email-deletion schedule.

Game storage remains on your device as described above. Providers control the retention of their own operational and security logs under their notices; the site does not set a single expiry period for those logs. Requests about information a provider handles independently may need to be addressed to that provider.

If you think information associated with this site has been exposed or misused, contact fromastoryteller@gmail.com with enough detail to investigate, without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Where a personal-data breach triggers legal notification duties, the affected people and relevant authorities must be notified as required.
