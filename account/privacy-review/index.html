Privacy — launch review

How this website handles information, what stays on your device, and the choices and rights available to you.

Launch review draft: 15 October 2026

Draft for review · account-review-2026-10-15-v2 · not the live policy

Preparing for viewer accounts

This is the proposed viewer-account notice for review, not the live policy. Signup, email verification, recovery, personal data downloads and confirmed deletion have been tested in FAS DEV. Public account registration remains closed. Review the remaining provider and operating arrangements before publishing this version. View policy versions and proposed signup wording.

Privacy statement

A simple overview

You can read stories and use the site without an account. The site has no visitor analytics or advertising trackers. Hosting and external resources still involve network requests, and the games save limited data in your browser.

Who is responsible

From A Storyteller is operated personally by Ryan Fearon, based in New Zealand. Ryan is responsible for information collected and held for this website. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests and complaints, email fromastoryteller@gmail.com.

Contact is by email. This notice covers fromastoryteller.com and correspondence about the site. It explains data practices; browsing or reading it does not give blanket consent to processing.

Information you choose to send

The Contact page provides an email address and links to social platforms. The site has no contact-submission form, newsletter subscription, public comments or payment checkout. Viewer accounts are proposed as described below.

If you email me, I receive your email address, any name you provide, your message and attachments, and the technical information included with the email. I use this information to answer your enquiry, discuss a collaboration you request, handle a privacy request or resolve a related issue. You do not have to send this information to browse the site, but I may be unable to answer an enquiry without enough detail or a reply address.

Please send only what is needed for your enquiry and avoid sending sensitive information or other people’s personal details unnecessarily. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. Email is handled through Gmail, a Google service; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Website delivery and search

The site is delivered through Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare receives IP addresses, requested URLs, request headers and necessary security/operational information to deliver and protect the service. The source repository and build checks use GitHub. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Site search matches content in your browser. A search term can appear in the page address as a query parameter and in your browser history; loading or sharing that address can disclose the term to the hosting service or the person receiving the link. Do not enter confidential information into site search. The site does not send search terms to a separate analytics service.

Viewer accounts and profiles

Accounts are intended for people aged 16 and over. Published content remains readable without an account. Creating an account does not give contributor or administrator privileges.

Creating an account requires your email, password, chosen display name, age-eligibility confirmation and the applicable policy acknowledgement/agreement. These details are used to create, secure and operate your account. A legal name is not required; profile bio is optional. If you do not provide the required account details, you cannot register but can keep reading public content. Authentication services process password hashes, verification/recovery information, sessions and necessary security records; passwords are not published or included in exports. The local test requires a 16+ self-declaration and acknowledgement of these draft notices. It stores the original declaration, notice versions and server timestamp separately from editable profile metadata; that record is included in personal account-data downloads and removed with the Auth identity. No birth date or identity document is collected by this test, and self-declaration does not verify actual age. This is not acceptance of a live account agreement. Final hosted age and Terms controls must be reviewed before registration opens.

The account test previews hold sign-in sessions in tab-scoped browser storage, with a memory-only fallback when storage is unavailable. This lets the tab retain a session during navigation; signing out clears its account session. Session credentials are necessary for account access and must not be shared. The hosted storage/expiry policy will be confirmed before launch. Email recovery credentials are held only in memory while completing recovery.

Your email is private account information. Display name and optional bio are profile information that may appear with published work. Do not put private contact details in those fields. Private drafts are limited to their author and authorised staff; published stories and approved public images can be read publicly.

Supabase provides authentication, database and uploaded-file storage. The FAS projects currently store primary project data in Sydney, Australia; provider support, security and other processing may occur elsewhere. Review the provider’s actual arrangements before launch. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy.

Account verification, password recovery and necessary security/service emails are transactional, not a marketing subscription. Resend is configured for development authentication email, using a verified sending domain with open/click tracking disabled. The domain sends from Tokyo; Resend states that account data, email metadata and logs are stored in the United States regardless of the sending region. Verification and recovery email delivery have been tested in DEV. Provider retention and the final production configuration still need review before public registration. See Resend’s Privacy Policy. Correspondence to the contact address continues through Gmail.

Account export and deletion — proposed

You can request access or correction using the contact address below. The local and DEV previews provide profile editing and a personal account-data ZIP for viewers and contributors, containing readable account details and a JSON copy for software. Contributor accounts can also download their owned Community stories as text files and their original images, with technical JSON in a separate metadata folder. Deletion requires password verification and a second confirmation. Existing static games/stories not linked to an account are outside that scope. Future saves/multiplayer need an export/deletion extension before launch. The account lifecycle is tested in DEV; it has not been enabled in production.

The proposed deletion process removes account and owned content from active systems after identity checks and confirmation. Deleted-account data may remain in restricted backups for up to 30 days, including manually saved archives, and must not be restored to active use. Only information genuinely required for a legal obligation, a dispute or necessary security handling may be retained separately for that purpose and no longer than needed. The operational schedule and restore procedure must be implemented before this commitment goes live.

Short-lived confirmation and request-limit records protect account actions. A minimal deletion record identifies the removed account, its status and dates so an older backup does not restore deleted material. Completed deletion receipts have a 30-day retention threshold; they do not contain the removed stories, images or passwords. The DEV cleanup schedule is installed and verified; the matching production schedule and backup process must be checked before public registration.

Public content can have been copied or independently archived by others; deletion from this service cannot promise removal of independent third-party copies. Service providers control their own independently required records under their notices; their actual retention must be checked before launch.

Future game saves and multiplayer — planning only

The current games store the limited local records described below. Cloud saves, account-linked scores and multiplayer are not enabled by this account preview.

If introduced, cloud saves may need an account ID, game and save-slot IDs, versioned progress, settings and update times. Multiplayer may need room membership, temporary moves/presence, participant IDs and match results. The exact data, visibility, security and retention will be explained before each feature starts. Chat, voice, location tracking and advertising data are not assumed.

Private saves should be visible only to their owner. Public leaderboards or shared match history need a clear visibility choice and must not expose account emails. Exports/deletion must cover game data without exposing or deleting another player’s private information. No consent to unspecified future collection is requested by this draft.

Game data stored on your device

If you allow Game records, Brick Blitz saves your all-time high score and Rally Clash saves your best rally. If you allow Preferences, the site saves your chosen colour theme and Rally Clash saves sound and master-volume preferences. These values use local storage in your browser for this site. The site reads and updates these values only while their category is allowed. No account is required for local game records.

The site’s game code does not send these saved values to me or to an online leaderboard. They are specific to your browser and device and are not linked to a site account. Local storage has no automatic expiry in the current games: values remain until overwritten, their category is turned off, you clear them, or your browser removes them. Once storage permission expires, they are not read or updated without a new choice.

To remove them, open your browser’s settings for cookies and site data, find fromastoryteller.com and clear its stored data. This resets saved records and preferences. Blocking site storage may prevent saving and can affect game operation. Browser storage is subject to privacy rules too; describing it here does not itself obtain consent.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current site code does not set HTTP cookies and does not include visitor analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural advertising, fingerprinting or session-recording tools. It does use the game storage described above. Ordinary browser caching and service-provider processing are separate from that storage.

The Cookies & site storage popup lets you allow optional storage, choose categories, or use essential storage only. Optional appearance/sound preferences and game records are not read or saved until you allow their category. Declining still lets you browse and play; current-page choices can remain in memory until you leave. Change your choice using Storage choices in the footer. Turning a category off removes its named saved values and stops future saving. Your choice is stored on this device for up to 180 days to honour it; clearing site data or an expired choice prompts you again. This does not delete account/cloud data. No analytics or advertising category is enabled. New optional processing requires a fresh assessment and any required consent; accepting Terms or browsing is not that consent.

Fonts, game libraries and external links

The site serves its MonteCarlo, Source Sans 3 and Literata fonts from its own assets. These font files do not require a separate request to Google Fonts.

Brick Blitz and Rally Clash load the p5.js and p5.sound libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare receives the network request information needed to deliver those files, including an IP address and request headers, and can process service logs. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. These game libraries are requested when the relevant page loads, independently of optional storage choices. A storage choice does not block network requests needed to deliver a page or game.

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are linked from the site. They are ordinary links, rather than embedded social feeds or video players in the current site. Following a link opens that provider’s service, where its own data practices apply. Their independent practices do not remove my responsibility for disclosures that I initiate.

Purposes and legal grounds

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, I rely on legitimate interests to answer ordinary correspondence, operate and secure this creative site, and address misuse or disputes, while considering your rights. Information needed to take steps towards a collaboration contract at your request can be processed for that purpose. Information required to meet a legal obligation is processed on that basis.

Where consent is required, it must be obtained separately for the particular purpose and can be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing. The provider notices linked above describe their own processing and legal grounds. I do not use your information for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects, or for profiling for those decisions.

Recipients and international processing

Information reaches the hosting, account, library-delivery and email providers described above to deliver those services. I do not sell or rent personal information, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Proposed account information is stored separately from correspondence, as described below. Correspondence may also need to be disclosed to comply with a lawful requirement or to obtain advice on a specific dispute, limited to what is necessary.

The site is available internationally. Correspondence is handled in New Zealand, and the providers above operate internationally, including in the United States and other countries. Information may therefore be processed outside your country, where privacy laws may differ. Their linked notices explain processing locations and transfer arrangements.

Where a transfer I make requires legal safeguards, the applicable transfer requirements must be met, for example through a recognised adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. You can contact me for information about the safeguards applicable to your information. Browsing this site does not waive your rights or amount to blanket consent to international transfers.

How long information is kept

Site-controlled authentication and security audit records are kept for up to 30 days, with longer retention only for a specific ongoing incident or legal need. Any such exception is restricted to that purpose and reviewed for deletion. This is separate from account records needed while your account remains open and from records independently retained by service providers.

My retention rule is to keep correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and relevant follow-up. An ongoing collaboration, an unresolved complaint, a legal obligation or a genuine need to establish or defend a claim can justify longer retention. When those purposes no longer apply, I delete the correspondence. This is a purpose-based retention rule; there is no automatic email-deletion schedule.

Game storage remains on your device as described above. Providers control the retention of their own operational and security logs under their notices; the site does not set a single expiry period for those logs. Requests about information a provider handles independently may need to be addressed to that provider.

Security

Please use the HTTPS version of this website. Transport encryption helps protect information in transit, but no website, email system or device can guarantee complete security. Keep your device protected, especially if other people can use the same browser or email account.

If you think information associated with this site has been exposed or misused, contact fromastoryteller@gmail.com with enough detail to investigate, without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Where a personal-data breach triggers legal notification duties, the affected people and relevant authorities must be notified as required.

Your privacy rights and choices

Contact fromastoryteller@gmail.com to ask what personal information I hold about you, request a copy or correction, or ask for deletion. Depending on the law that applies and the circumstances, you may also have rights to restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, receive portable data, withdraw consent, or opt out of a sale, sharing or targeted advertising. The site currently does not conduct those sale, sharing or advertising activities.

I will consider requests regardless of where you live. Legal rights and exceptions vary: for example, information may need to be retained for a legal obligation or claim. If a request cannot be granted, I will explain why and any available complaint or appeal route. You will not be treated unfairly for exercising your rights.

Please identify the correspondence or information involved. I may request proportionate information to verify your identity or an authorised representative’s authority. Do not send identity documents unless requested. Requests will be handled as soon as reasonably practicable. For requests under New Zealand privacy law, the normal response deadline is 20 working days; any lawful extension will be explained. Account downloads are a convenient option and do not replace your right to request other information I hold, including relevant correspondence. There is normally no charge, except where the law permits one.

A Global Privacy Control signal expresses a preference to opt out of sale or sharing in jurisdictions recognising it. The current site has no such sale or sharing to turn off; the signal does not clear local game data. You can remove that data through your browser as described above.

Complaints

You can raise a privacy concern with fromastoryteller@gmail.com. You may also complain to the privacy or data-protection regulator with authority over your complaint, following its complaint process. Some regulators, including the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner, normally expect you to try resolving the issue with the organisation first. Nothing here restricts your statutory complaint rights.

Relevant authorities can include the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner, the UK Information Commissioner, or the supervisory authority in the EEA country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. If applicable law provides an appeal against a refused rights request, email me to request a review; you retain any right to contact the relevant regulator.

Children and young visitors

Public browsing does not require an account. The proposed account policy is 16+, subject to the applicable requirements for the actual audience and features. Some stories contain mature themes and may not suit younger readers. A parent or guardian can contact me about personal information a child has sent by email.

Please do not send a child’s personal information unnecessarily. If information has been collected in circumstances requiring parental authorisation or other protection, it must be deleted or handled with the safeguards required by applicable law. This statement does not serve as parental consent.
