Arcade: Rally Clash

A fast-paced paddle duel where every rally matters.

Take on the computer in this Pong-inspired browser arcade game. Stay sharp, return the ball, and be the first to reach 5 points.

How to Play

Return the ball, keep rallies alive, and outplay the computer. Every point counts, and the first to 5 points wins the match.

Controls

  • Use the arrows and Enter or your mouse to navigate the menus.
  • Use the Up and Down arrows to move the left paddle.
  • Press Escape to pause.

About the Game

Arcade: Rally Clash is a Pong-inspired browser arcade game and the first game I built for From A Storyteller while learning the art of game development. It focuses on quick matches and satisfying rally-based gameplay.
