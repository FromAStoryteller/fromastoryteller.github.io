Arcade: Brick Blitz

By Ry

Smash through the wall of bricks and keep the ball alive.

Take on a fast-paced brick breaker challenge in this browser arcade game. Clear the bricks and see how long you can stay alive.

ArcadeSingle player

How to Play

Bounce the ball off your paddle and smash through the brick wall. Keep the ball alive, clear as many bricks as you can.

Controls
  • Use the arrow keys and Enter, or your mouse, to navigate the menus.
  • Use the Left and Right Arrow keys to move the paddle.
  • Press Escape to pause.
  • Tap the menu options to navigate.
  • Touch and drag left or right on the game to move the paddle.
  • Tap the Pause button to pause.

About the Game

Arcade: Brick Blitz is a Breakout-inspired browser arcade game built by From A Storyteller. It follows Rally Clash as part of the arcade collection and focuses on fast reactions, satisfying rebounds, and chasing a highscore.
