Countdown

Set a focused countdown timer for your writing, coding, planning, or creative work.

Choose an amount of time, press start, then work on your project until the timer reaches zero.

Countdown

00:00:00

Set your time, then start the countdown.

How It Works

Set the number of hours, minutes, and seconds you want to count down from. When you press start, the timer will update every second until it reaches zero.

Why Use It

Great for focused writing sessions, coding blocks, planning time, quick creative sprints, or giving yourself a clear window to make progress on a project.
