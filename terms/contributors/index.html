Contributor policy

These contributor rules are part of the Terms. They apply when you upload, edit or publish content, including writing, covers, profile images and metadata. Contributor access is by invitation or an administrator’s role assignment; registration alone does not enable publishing. Current Studio features support the content types shown there, not every type available elsewhere on the site.

Your work and the permission you give

You retain ownership of your work. You may publish it elsewhere and choose a pseudonym. You must own the material or have the permissions needed for its use here, including writing, images, quotations and any identifiable person’s information. Credit and attribution do not replace permission. Honour third-party licences and any attribution they require.

By submitting material, you grant From A Storyteller a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to store, back up, reproduce, technically format and display it as needed to operate this website. Service providers acting for the site may process it for those purposes. Technical formatting includes resizing or cropping images as you select, accessible presentation and delivery across devices; it does not transfer ownership or give staff permission to rewrite your story.

Drafts remain private under the access rules in the Privacy notice. Choosing Publish additionally permits public display of the work, its selected author name, cover, summary and metadata, and use in this site’s listings, search and link previews. This permission does not authorise selling your work, licensing it to unrelated publishers, using it to train AI models, or separate promotional reuse outside normal page links and previews. Such uses require a separate agreement. No exclusive publishing rights are requested.

You can end public display by unpublishing, archiving or deleting through the available controls. The permission to display ends when the work is removed from public service; limited storage permission continues for retained drafts, operational removal and restricted backups, or a specific lawful retention need described in the Privacy notice. Independently made copies, search caches and shared previews may remain outside the site’s control. A third-party licence you separately granted may have its own continuing terms.

Publishing and responsibility

Contributors publish without prior approval, including image uploads. You are responsible for checking the work, cover crop, image description, attribution, content warnings, genres and required metadata before publishing. Published edits can become public immediately. Scheduled publication remains private until its scheduled release succeeds. Preview your work and keep your own copy; autosave and browser recovery cannot guarantee preservation.

Do not publish unlawful or infringing material, plagiarism, impersonation, fraud, malicious code, spam, threats, targeted harassment, hateful abuse, sexual exploitation or abuse of children, non-consensual intimate material, or private personal information without a lawful basis. Fiction may explore difficult subjects, but a warning does not make prohibited material acceptable. Use accurate content warnings and request a missing warning through Studio or support. Do not mislead readers about authorship or rights, including for AI-assisted material; assistance does not remove your responsibility for what you publish.

Only use an organisation’s publishing identity if the site has authorised it. A displayed FAS name does not transfer your copyright or remove your responsibility. Tools use the site’s organisation identity. Your optional social links are public links; use only accounts or websites you are entitled to represent.

Moderation and review

Staff may restrict access, remove contributor access, unpublish or archive material to address these rules, safety, rights complaints, security or legal obligations. No prior approval is promised or required. Staff moderation does not make the site the author of your work. Staff may review private material only as needed to operate, support or protect the service and investigate a relevant issue; drafts are not public submissions for general staff reading.

Where reasonably practicable, we will explain a restriction and its reason through account information or contact with you. Urgent protective action may happen first, and details may be withheld to protect people, an investigation or a legal obligation. To report a problem or request a review, email fromastoryteller@gmail.com with the page URL, the issue, relevant evidence and the outcome requested. Avoid passwords and unnecessary personal details. You may explain or correct a mistake; staff will consider the request and communicate the outcome. No response time or restoration is guaranteed, and statutory complaint rights remain available.

Leaving, removal and future payments

Unpublish returns your work to a private draft. Author archiving removes it from public listings and allows restoration to a draft; a staff moderation lock needs staff review. Never-published drafts can be deleted; previously published work must use the available archive and delete flow. Destructive actions require confirmation. Download your content before deletion. Public URLs and page state follow the work’s lifecycle; changing a title does not promise a new public address.

Account deletion removes the account and its linked, owned Community content and uploaded images from active systems. Static site files outside account ownership, another person’s independent work, restricted backups and limited lawful records need the treatment explained in the Privacy notice. Contact support if self-service deletion is unavailable, including for administrator accounts, or for a rights/removal concern involving static material. Privacy requests, exports and deletion do not require acceptance of later Terms.

Current contributions are unpaid. There is no promise of views, advertising income or future monetisation. Revenue sharing, paid features, advertising arrangements or additional licences need clear separate terms before participation; accepting these rules does not enrol you in them. Any later material change to these contributor rules will be versioned and presented for fresh agreement before further contributions require it.
